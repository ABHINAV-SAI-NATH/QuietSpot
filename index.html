    Not measuring.

    1. Stand or sit where you would actually work.
    2. Hold the phone or laptop still and stay quiet.
    3. Allow microphone access when your browser asks.
    4. Take it two or three times at different hours.

    Noise guide

    What the numbers mean for reading, writing and thinking.

    20 to 30 dB

    Whisper quiet

    Archive rooms, empty chapels, a garden before the town wakes up. Good for slow reading and anything that needs your full attention.

    30 to 42 dB

    Library calm

    Pages turning, soft footsteps, a distant hum. Suits long reading sessions, writing and revision.

    42 to 52 dB

    Gentle murmur

    A café with low talk and soft music. Fine for sketching, coding, and loose first drafts.

    • Go early. Most places are at their quietest in the first hour after opening.
    • Sit away from doors. Entrances, counters and printers are where the sound comes from.
    • Carry earplugs. They do more than headphones for a lot less money.
    • Check the policy. Rooms with a stated quiet rule stay quiet; rooms without one drift.
