CP6 Usability Audit & Fixes

  • ISS-01 (Floor Card Hold CTA): Students in user tests tapped floor headers expecting to reserve. Added high-contrast primary "Hold Best Quiet Seat (15 Min)" buttons directly on every floor card.
  • ISS-02 (Scan Desk QR Discoverability): Physical check-in was buried in nested menus. Elevated "Scan Desk QR" into a persistent top nav pill + bottom mobile nav bar.
  • ISS-03 (Power Socket Visibility): Laptops dying during study sprints caused drop-offs. Added a dedicated "⚡ Power Outlets Only" filter chip and visual socket badges on desks.
  • ISS-04 (Decorum Anonymity Fear): Students feared retaliation or discipline if their ID was shown. Enforced 100% anonymous reporting with a prominent trust badge and zero credential inputs.
